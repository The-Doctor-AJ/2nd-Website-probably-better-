Liminal Spaces plus stories of my own!

Published on August, 2025 by AJ

Liminal Spaces Hey everyone!! It's been a while since I last posted a blog. But this time it's not really about media or anything like that. Noo, I've been wanting to talk about the wonders of Liminal spaces. Ever since the trend popped into existence, I've been fascinated with them, and I still am to this day. I wanted to make this blog to at least share the ones that I like looking at. When I stare into them, I always have a scenario that pops into my head, and I've always envisioned myself in that Liminal Space. Though most of the time, the Liminal Spaces that I like to look at are usually malls that were once thriving with people, or stores that I swear I've been there in real life.


I haven't been to this place, but I sure feel like I have been. But I actually have been to a "closed down" mall before, and I'm using air quotes because only at least two stores were still open in that mall for whatever reason, but when I was walking down the closed mall, it was exactly like this. No lights, no AC, and even the plants were tipped over. I wasn't really scared of the place, but I was feeling a bit uncertain as I was walking through the mall; it felt like I was stepping into a whole other forgotten world in there, and in that mall was just what was left of it. It's a true story. Source: Trust me, bro Another Liminal Space that I like looking at is usually from video games, but I'm not talking about the newer stuff, more so about older games. Like N64 type of video games, but there are some exceptions if someone ever introduces me to them, and they have really eerie and creepy Liminal spaces. But regardless, the Liminal Spaces that I like looking at in Video Games are usually the Super Mario 64 Liminal Spaces. Despite that game being older than I am, I grew up with that game when I was a kid, so I have an attachment to that game even today. But you do have to admit that game does have some eerie things about it.
