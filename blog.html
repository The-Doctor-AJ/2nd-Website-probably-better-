Tomodachi Life 2 update

Welcome one and all to another virtual paper with words on it. Last time I wrote about Tomodachi Life, it was about the demo. If you haven’t read it, well you can always read it, its still up on my website and on here as well or I can always do a TLDR type of thing here, which I will now. TLDR basically, I shared my thoughts on how faithful the demo was to the original demo from the 3DS era and on how much is already improved in the game. But now since it has been a few weeks now I can give an update about the full game!

Well for one, there is a lot more things to do than how the original was back then. But only 1 glaring issue, at least from my experience on how the mii interact with one another. And thats kinda the problem, they rarely do somewhat. Back on the 3DS, when two miis meet, they’ll want to become friends fast, but with here, they love to take their sweet him with being friend. Its goes faster when miis become lovers than they do friends, again at least from my experience.

Another glaring issue, is the lack of a concert hall. I’m optimistic and hoping that in a future update they’ll bring it back so we can have longer songs than how it was back on the original, but so far is just me hoping. Yeah the drawing is fun and all but even then that can be a bit overwhelming when I’m given charge of creating new things to put on my island, so far I only have about 37 miis on my island, and so far everyone seems to be getting along, though thats also a bit of a problem.

Back on my 3DS island, everyday I got on to check on the miis, everyone would be pissed as each other, and honestly, I kinda miss that, at first I found it annoying but now I kinda miss it. Another thing, babies in the game. I can’t help my miis take care of their off-spring like how it was on the 3DS. Back on the 3DS, we were allowed to help our miis to take care of their kids, but now its just a cutscene of the kid mii growing. As annoying as that rocking mini game was back on the 3DS, I feel like I took it for granted, now I kinda want it back.

This isn’t a blog talking about how good the original was, farr from that. I love this game and I think Living the Dream is actually a wayy better game than the original, but I just wish I could have all the cool and wacky stuff from the original onto this one, but at least a little different.

But thats enough about the game itself, whats going on my island? Well so far, again I added around 37 miis on the island and everyone seems to be getting long. How ever they really like to put a bunch of shit of looks nice but in the wrong places. Literally we have flower beds and bells on the freakin side walk, and everytime they ask its always going to be something they wanna put and I’d have to fix it. But so far my island is around level 156, yah, this game took a bunch of my time, time I could of put towards my college education, but like nahhhh.

So far I seem to have unlocked everything from the wishing fountain, and I still have yet unlock everything, and honestly I still need to add more miis, but I ran out of idea of what miis to add. I tried to add some from the Tomodachi share site, but the drawings on there are amazing but too detailed for my skill level of drawing, so I just avoided that part of the share site. And only about 3 couples are married I believe, I decided to fill in space and just add my IRL self into the island. No way there was going to be more miis I would be able to add without trying to. I already added some friends online, but now I just need to fill in space. But anyway, so far only two married families actually had kids and now their getting their own relationships which is great!

I guess thats all I can say about the island, I can try to update y’all as soon as I get more time into the game, especially since the semester is almost over. So maybe y’all can possibly get your miis onto my island! Maybe maybe, who knows, I don’t :P

I can also say how annoying getting pictures from my switch is? Its really annoying how I always have to turn it off and take out the sd card just so I can get the photos, why can’t I just have it sent to my phoneee!

Thats bullshit!!

Until next time everyone, goodbye

My thoughts and ratings of: Young Indiana Jones

Welcome all to another piece of virtual paper with words on it. If some of y'all would know(probably not all), but I have been taking a break from watching Doctor Who since its a long show and a lot of things to remember, and honestly the thought of watching it again it fun and all but I just need a break from watching all of the adventures that the Doctor has. As for my next hyperfixation, it was a bit unexpected but I had a feeling it was going to come back at some point in my life.

For a while I have been a big fan of the Indiana Jones movies and that's still the case with me today. They're good movies and full of more adventures that would seem a bit more grounded? Well I wouldn't say it's grounded, but it has my interest in history even though those movies aren't exactly historically accurate, I still like the idea of a guy finding all these ancient artifacts as he tries to beat his competitors to it. And after watching the trilogy again, I did feel satisfied but it felt like I needed more than just the 3 movies. Of course there are the 2 other squeals, but like, we don't talk about them…

However, just one night after watching some youtube one day, a video came up on my recommendation and it was a memory core that was unlocked and that was when I decided to watch it all. It was The Young Indiana Jones Chronicles. I remember the show, it was mostly because I actually have one of the episodes on VHS from a long time ago but it was just that one episode and I actually never thought about seeing the rest of the show. Out of my own curiosity, I decided that was going to be the next show I was going to watch and finish and I had ventured out to seek out the show.

There always is the usual streaming services, but after doing the research I felt pain paying how much I paid just to get the DVDs copies. How much did I pay for the series on DVD? One….hundard and sixty two dollars.. Honestly, it was kinda worth it, seeing how rare the volumes are, especially the 2nd volume. Since most of the DVD and I'm guessing the 2nd volume of Young Indy is out of print, that would explain the high price on it own, and seeing volume 1 and 3 on how reasonably priced they are, I would saying it would be better to get the european release instead of the american one, but it was all sold out so I had to cut my losses and just pay the 162 dollars. Still pisses me off since I found out that someone UPLOADED THE ENTIRE SHOW ON YOUTUBE! FOR FREE! But that was way after I had gotten the DVDs in my possession.


But what do I think of the show? Well for one the DVDs are pretty bulky for a 2 season show. Honestly I thought it was 3 seasons, but after doing the research, that wasn't the case, there was supposed to be a 3rd season but sadly it was canceled back in 1993. But the show it's actually not that bad of a show. This show was supposed to be slightly educational, as it goes through Indiana Jones for when he was a kid all the way until he was a young adult during the early 1900s until 1920. Though the show is directed by George Lucas so you can expect some inaccuracies during the show, but then again when was any historical show/movies 100% accurate(Please don't answer this question, I wanna feel smart about this :P)
And sadly if you do know the reputation that George Lucas has with his movies. Editing and removing scenes from his movies, this is no different. Even Indiana Jones wasn't safe from that. During the original broadcasting, there was supposed to be an old man Indy living with his daughter and grandson, and old man Indy was supposed to be a story teller telling the events of the episode. But the only reason why george lucas removed old man Indy from the show was because of the 4th movie, Indiana Jones and Kingdom of the crystal skull, which after seeing both the show and the 4th movie, there would of been some inconsistency in the lore, so I guess it makes sense, but crystal skull deserve its own blog.


The only way you would be able to see old man Indy is to find the original broadcasting or youtube and just watch the old man Indy scenes and then watch the episodes afterwards. But even if the show is educational it definitely sparked my interest in learning more about the events that show covers, which brings me to my favorite episodes.

    My Top 5 from least to best would of been
  • Trenches of Hell 1916 and Love's sweet song 1916
  • Spring Break Adventure 1916
  • Treasure of the Peacock's Eye 1919 (This is the episode I had on VHS a long time ago)
  • Adventures in the Secret Service 1917
  • Mystery of the Blues 1920

I would say these are my favorite episodes from the show and number 1 being my all time favorite. For one Mystery of the blues is possibly the most popular episode for just one reason. They actually got the original actor for Indiana Jones to also star in the episode, though he was barely in the episode, I still enjoyed the episode. Talking about Jazz, the Blues and a bit of murder mystery(spoilers) so it's kinda like the Blink episode from Doctor Who where 10th was barely in the episode but it had good story telling.

There are a few things I wish the show did, like for one keeping old man Indy, with the DVDs it does have special features, so george lucas could of saved old man Indy and put him in the deleted scene section but I guess we didn't get enough documentaries in the DVDs, which is fine but I still wished they saved him. Another thing I wish was if Harrison Ford(the original actor for Indy)could of narrated Indy and his adventures, it would of been cool, if Ford was able to narrate in the original Blade Runner then he could of done this too.
But if you don't mind the education part of the show then I would say give the show a chance, it should be uploaded on youtube, its like literally someone ripped the DVDs and put them up on youtube, which is what you're going to get. It is on amazon prime too but you have to pay for the episodes which is kinda annoying.
But my overall ratings of the show would be an 8/10. Just for the fact that I do like history and liked doing the research on the side, it made me love the show. It's a piece of Indiana Jones history that I hope it doesn't get forgotten for too long.
Thank you for reading! :D

My thoughts on the new Tomodachi Life 2 Demo

Published on March 27th 2026

As my 2nd blog on here, I wanted to give my thoughts on the new Tomodachi life demo. Even though it's only a demo, I still feel like I wanted to give my thoughts on it. I do hope it can fill in the shoes of the original on the 3DS. Please keep in mind that I haven't gotten alot of time in the game, but I still wanted to write my thoughts and compare it to the original, I can't fully do that with the demo. However this demo offers a little more than the original demo. This is just my opinion! :D

During the time of when I played the original Tomodachi, at least the demo version, it was kinda the same as here with the new demo. Not a lot was available but it was enough to entertain me for a while, even though the demo only had like a few outfits available on the it was fun to just add mii to its limit and have everyone wear the same outfits. And the mii limit of the new demo is actually the same from the original. Only 3 miis are allowed to be on the island.

Though the only difference is that there is more clothing options and the fact that we're able to now mix and match outfits, it's a huge improvement from the original. While I did like the outfits in the original, I always thought that it would of been better if I was able to mix and match outfits instead of having a pre-made outfit available, which on that note, pre-made outfits are available as well! For now there isn't alot to do (at least with the amount of time I have been playing) But I do like the amount of interactivity that I was able to have when being able to watch the miis socialize. For example, my main mii wanted to make friends with another mii and asked what they should talk about. I was surprised that I was able to actually give them a custom topic, so of course, I typed Doctor Who, and it ended up working. They both became friends! :D

A new thing was the wishes, I didn't understand it at first, but when I got the 2nd wish, I somewhat started to understand it a little more, which I still kinda don't but I'm starting to see it as when miis leveled up in the original. However this is a little more different than how it was in the original, while happiness level is still in this demo, there are things that have to be unlocked with the wishes (at least from what I understand from it). Another thing that I wanted to write down was from the mii customization. Its definitely wayyy better than the original. Since its still a demo, it messed up my miis head so it looks a little bit odd, but I was happy enough to go with it. Though the only thing I thought was hard (for me at least) was the voices screen. This part isn't as customizable as the mii looks but I was able to alot of cool thing that the original wasn't able to do. Though the voices are gonna be different from what I had in the original, I can live with it, but I still kinda prefer the original and how they handled the voices.
At the end the game said that I wasn't able to earn more money or get their levels up, which is fine, I do wish I was able to earn more money so that I can play dress up with the miis, but that's just gonna make me wait until the full game releases. I can't wait for the full release! Here are some pictures to go with it! Not alot of pics but like I enjoyed the demo alot! :D
